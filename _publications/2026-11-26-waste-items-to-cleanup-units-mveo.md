**Authors:** Y. Bardes, H. Soubra, Z. Noumir, A. Ramdane-Chérif.

Waste datasets disagree on what one object is: a single bottle, or a whole pile to clean up. We build reviewed relational annotations for 1,891 images from 15 ground, UAV and CCTV sources, and develop a geometry-first method with visual correction that turns waste-item boxes into cleanup units.

Training on the harmonized labels raises detection mAP@0.5 from 0.434 to 0.563, against 0.610 with manual reference labels.
