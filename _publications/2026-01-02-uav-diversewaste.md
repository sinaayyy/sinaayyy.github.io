**Authors:** Y. Bardes, A. Tounsi, H. Soubra, Z. Noumir, A. Ramdane-Chérif.

UAV-DiverseWaste consolidates 24 third-party datasets and an Unreal Engine 5 drone simulation into a 30,387-image resource for aerial waste detection. It comes with D²EW, a 2,442-image audited benchmark. Pooling the sources nearly doubled accuracy on an unseen test campaign.
