I am a PhD candidate in Artificial Intelligence at [Université Paris-Saclay](https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/) (LISV laboratory), in an industrial CIFRE doctorate with **Alten Labs**. My research is on **federated object detection for UAVs under hardware constraints**: which detectors can be trained on board, how to adapt them across clients with very different data, and how to learn when clients have no labels. The application is real-time waste detection by drone.

**I am looking for a research internship of up to 6 months starting in January 2027** (PhD defense planned after the internship). Feel free to [contact me](mailto:yanis.bardes@gmail.com).

I am supervised by Amar Ramdane-Chérif (LISV), Hassan Soubra (LyRIDS, ECE Paris) and Zineb Noumir (Alten Labs). I also took graduate courses in the [MVA Master](https://www.master-mva.com/) at ENS Paris-Saclay.

Alongside my research, I teach at [ECE Paris](https://www.ece.fr/) (Artificial Intelligence, MLOps, Mathematics) and [ESIEE Paris](https://www.esiee.fr/) (Big Data, Full-Stack Data Applications), including master's-level courses taught in English.

- Datasets, annotation harmonization and benchmarks
- Large language models and RAG
